## 6. Sustainability Across the 7 Performance Domains (PMBOK 8)

| Domain | Sustainability Action |
|---|---|
| **Governance** | ESG review at every Steering meeting; ethics escalation path |
| **Stakeholders** | Identify & engage marginalized voices; transparent reporting |
| **Scope** | Build sustainability into acceptance criteria (e.g., energy efficiency targets) |
| **Schedule** | Avoid burnout; sustainable pace; no false urgency |
| **Finance** | Cost models include carbon, lifecycle, externality costs |
| **Resources** | Diverse hiring; psychological safety; ethical supplier sourcing |
| **Risk** | Climate risk, regulatory ESG risk, social license risk |

## 7. Sustainable Procurement Requirements

All vendors and suppliers must:
- [ ] Provide carbon footprint data for products/services delivered
- [ ] Sign Supplier Code of Conduct
- [ ] Demonstrate fair labor practices
- [ ] Meet [organizational ESG threshold]
- [ ] Disclose any sub-tier supplier risks

## 8. Reporting & Disclosure

| Report | Frequency | Audience | Standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| ESG Status (in Project Status Report) | Weekly | Sponsor | Internal |
| Sustainability Report | Monthly | Steering | GRI / SASB / TCFD aligned |
| End-of-project ESG Report | Project close | Org leadership + external | Aligned with org ESG report |

---

## 9. Compliance Checklist

- [ ] All material ESG issues identified and assessed
- [ ] Sustainability acceptance criteria added to each major deliverable
- [ ] Carbon accounting tool selected and operational
- [ ] Supplier code of conduct signed by all vendors
- [ ] WCAG / accessibility requirements in scope
- [ ] DEI considerations in team composition and decision-making
- [ ] Climate risks added to risk register
- [ ] ESG metrics in monthly status reports
