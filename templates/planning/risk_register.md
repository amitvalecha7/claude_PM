## Risk Probability-Impact Scale

| Rating | Probability | Impact (Cost) | Impact (Schedule) | Impact (Scope/Quality) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **5 — Very High** | >70% | >20% budget overrun | >4 weeks delay | Critical deliverable affected |
| **4 — High** | 51–70% | 10–20% overrun | 2–4 weeks delay | Major deliverable affected |
| **3 — Medium** | 31–50% | 5–10% overrun | 1–2 weeks delay | Moderate impact |
| **2 — Low** | 11–30% | 2–5% overrun | 3–5 days delay | Minor impact |
| **1 — Very Low** | ≤10% | <2% overrun | <3 days delay | Negligible |

**Risk Score = Probability × Impact**
- 15–25: **Critical** (Red) — Immediate response required
- 8–14: **High** (Orange) — Active management required
- 4–7: **Medium** (Yellow) — Monitor and prepare contingency
- 1–3: **Low** (Green) — Accept and monitor

## Risk Categories (Risk Breakdown Structure)

```
Project Risks
├── Technical Risks
│   ├── Technology complexity
│   ├── Integration issues
│   └── Performance requirements
├── Schedule Risks
│   ├── Resource availability
│   ├── Dependency delays
│   └── Estimation errors
├── Budget/Cost Risks
│   ├── Scope creep
│   ├── Vendor cost changes
│   └── Currency / inflation
├── Resource Risks
│   ├── Key person dependency
│   ├── Skill gaps
│   └── Team turnover
├── External Risks
│   ├── Regulatory changes
│   ├── Market conditions
│   └── Stakeholder changes
└── Organizational Risks
    ├── Priority changes
    ├── Funding cuts
    └── Governance issues
```
