> - We attend all scheduled meetings or notify the PM in advance
> - We document decisions and action items
> - We celebrate wins and learn from mistakes

## Team Development Plan (Tuckman's Stages)

| Stage | Activities | PM Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Forming** | Kickoff, team charter, introductions | Set expectations, build safety |
| **Storming** | Role conflicts, process disagreements | Facilitate resolution, enforce norms |
| **Norming** | Established rhythm, collaboration | Reinforce positive behaviors |
| **Performing** | High output, self-managing | Remove blockers, celebrate wins |
| **Adjourning** | Project close, recognition | Recognize contributions, lessons learned |

## Resource Conflict Resolution

1. **Priority matrix:** High-priority project tasks take precedence over low-priority
2. **Escalation:** Resource conflicts escalate to Sponsor after 48 hours unresolved
3. **Leveling:** PM applies resource leveling before crashing schedule
