# Communication Policy
**PMBOK Reference:** 10.1 Plan Communications Management | Knowledge Area: Communications Management

---

## Purpose

This policy ensures consistent, timely, accurate, and appropriate communication across all project stakeholders. Effective communication is a primary responsibility of the Project Manager.

---

## Communication Principles

1. **Timely** — Information is shared when needed, not after the fact
2. **Accurate** — All communications reflect current project reality (no sugar-coating)
3. **Appropriate** — Right message, right audience, right channel, right time
4. **Documented** — Key communications are recorded for the project record
5. **Transparent** — Issues and risks are communicated proactively, not hidden
6. **Two-way** — Communication is not just broadcasting; feedback is actively sought

## Status Reporting Standards

### RAG Status Definitions

| Color | Meaning | Action Required |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **Green** | On track; no significant issues | None — continue monitoring |
| 🟡 **Amber** | At risk; corrective action in progress; may require sponsor attention | PM to provide corrective action plan |
| 🔴 **Red** | Off track; immediate action required; escalation triggered | Sponsor decision required; recovery plan needed |

**Rule:** The PM must never change a status from Red/Amber to Green without evidence that the issue is actually resolved.

### Status Report Deadlines

- **Weekly Status Report:** Submitted every **Friday by 5:00 PM**
- **Monthly Steering Report:** Submitted **2 business days before** the Steering meeting
- **Late reports:** PM notifies sponsor by noon on due date if report will be late

---

## Meeting Standards

### Before Every Meeting
- [ ] Agenda sent ≥ 24 hours in advance
- [ ] Materials distributed ≥ 24 hours in advance
- [ ] Attendance confirmed

### During Every Meeting
- [ ] Start on time (wait max 2 minutes for latecomers)
- [ ] Note-taker assigned
- [ ] Action items recorded with owner and due date
- [ ] Decisions documented

### After Every Meeting
- [ ] Minutes distributed within 24 hours
- [ ] Action items entered in Action Item Log
- [ ] Next meeting confirmed

## Communication Channel Guidelines

| Channel | Use For | Response SLA |
|---|---|---|
| **Email** | Formal communications, status reports, decisions, CRs | 24 hours |
| **Instant Message (Slack/Teams)** | Quick questions, informal coordination | 2–4 hours during business hours |
| **Phone / Video Call** | Urgent issues, escalations, sensitive matters | Immediate / scheduled ASAP |
| **Project Portal / PMIS** | Document sharing, dashboards, formal records | N/A |
| **In-person Meeting** | Complex discussions, decisions, sensitive topics | Scheduled |

---

## Stakeholder-Specific Communication Rules

| Stakeholder | Rules |
|---|---|
| **Sponsor** | Never surprise the Sponsor. Share bad news early. Keep 1:1 weekly. |
| **Steering Committee** | Provide executive summary; focus on decisions needed; no excessive detail. |
| **Customer** | All formal commitments in writing. No verbal scope agreements. |
| **Team** | Be transparent about project health. Share the "why" behind decisions. |
| **Vendors** | All communications documented. Contract changes in writing only. |

---

## Confidentiality Rules

| Information Type | Classification | Shareable With |
|---|---|---|
| Financial data (budget, costs) | Confidential | PM, Sponsor, Finance, CCB |
| Risk register (full) | Confidential | PM, Sponsor, Team Leads |
| HR / performance issues | Restricted | PM, HR, direct manager only |
| Technical architecture | Internal | Project team, approved vendors |
| Status reports (general) | Internal | Approved distribution list |
| Project Charter | Internal | All stakeholders |

---

## Communication Failure Escalation

If a stakeholder fails to respond within SLA:
1. **24h:** PM sends a follow-up message on alternate channel
2. **48h:** PM calls the stakeholder directly
3. **72h+:** PM escalates to Sponsor per `policies/escalation.md`
